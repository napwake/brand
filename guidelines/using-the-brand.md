# Using the brand

For talks, posts, videos, slides and projects that work with Napwake. The [trademark policy](../TRADEMARKS.md) covers the name and logo, and the [illustration licence](../illustrations/LICENSE) covers the doorman illustrations.

## The words

- **Headline:** Non-prod environments that sleep when nobody's using them, and wake when someone does.
- **Plain line,** under the headline, or alone where space is short: Pauses idle Kubernetes workloads in staging, preview and dev, and wakes them on the first request.
- **What it won't do:** It never deletes a workload or its data.
- **The number,** always with its assumption: A team that works 10 hours a day, five days a week, uses staging 50 of 168 hours. The other 70%, it's idle.

Quote them as they are. If you write your own, the six voice rules in the [guidelines](README.md#voice) are what make it sound like Napwake.

## Talks and slides

- The horizontal or stacked logo, on Paper or Night, from [`../logo/`](../logo/).
- Headlines in Big Shoulders Display ExtraBold, body in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, commands in Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, all free on Google Fonts.
- One idea per slide. Illustrations from the [poses](../illustrations/poses/) and [scenes](../illustrations/scenes/), with the [finish](doorman.md#the-finish).
- Show real `kubectl napwake` output: trim columns if you need to, never invent them.

## Posts, articles and videos

- Credit the illustrations: "Doorman illustrations by Napwake, CC BY 4.0", linked to https://github.com/napwake/brand.
- "Works with Napwake" and "for Napwake" are fine for your project. A name or logo that looks like Napwake's isn't.
- When you compare Napwake with other tools, say plainly where another fits better. That's how we write about them too.
